What to keep in mind

  • The data is frozen. The source, Transfermarkt datasets, is no longer updated.
  • It covers 14 European leagues, league games only, from 2012. "The other games" does not mean a whole career.
  • Romania's Superliga is missing, because the source has no player data for it.
  • Patterns show what happened, not why. More wins with a player starting do not prove the player caused them.
  • Numbers are recounted from the source when the feed is built, so a mistake in the source would show up here too.
